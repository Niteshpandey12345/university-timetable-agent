# ITC-2007 Track 3 (curriculum-based course timetabling) instances comp01–comp21

Public benchmark of the Second International Timetabling Competition (Di Gaspero, McCollum & Schaerf, 2007).
Files copied from Tomáš Müller's repository: https://github.com/tomas-muller/cpsolver-itc2007 (data/ctt/).
Best-known bounds (UD2) are from Ceschia, Di Gaspero & Schaerf (2022/2023), Table 13 (arXiv:2201.07525),
and should be re-checked on OptHub (https://opthub.uniud.it) before publication.
